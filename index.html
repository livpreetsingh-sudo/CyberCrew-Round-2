Our Origins & Philosophy

Meet Our Founder & Principal

The story of a Harvard scholar who returned home to Bhopal to build the school Indian children truly deserve.

Dr. Harshvardhan Shrivastva

PhD in Education Policy, Harvard University • Founder & Principal

“What is the use of knowing how great schools work if I never build one for my own people?”

After spending 17 formative years in the United States advising progressive school districts, training educators, and publishing peer-reviewed research on child cognitive development at Harvard University, Dr. Harshvardhan Shrivastva observed an undeniable truth: schools succeed not through rigid standardized drills, but by honoring the unique curiosity of each child.

Every trip back home to India brought him face to face with exceptionally bright, eager youngsters who possessed immense natural talent but lacked learning spaces equipped to cultivate it holistically.

“I understand classrooms, curricula, and child psychology. I do not understand vanity websites. Parents will judge my school by this website long before they meet me in person. It must feel professional, warm, and trustworthy—because their child's future is our sacred trust.”

Endeavour International School is the culmination of this lifelong conviction. Established in Bhopal, affiliated with CISCE, EIS bridges world-class evidence-based pedagogy with India's deep cultural ethos, ensuring our students think globally while remaining grounded in empathy and integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions parents ask about our campus, curriculum, and admissions.

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) is globally celebrated for its equal weighting of science, mathematics, humanities, and deep mastery of the English language. Unlike rote-heavy curricula, ICSE (at Class 10) builds comprehensive analytical and written communication skills, while ISC (at Class 12) prepares students exceptionally well for both top Indian competitive examinations (JEE, NEET, CUET, CLAT) and premier international universities.

In standard classrooms with 40 to 50 children, quiet or unique learners often slip through the cracks. At Endeavour, our strict ceiling of 25 students per class and 1:15 ratio guarantees that every educator knows your child personally, tracks their individual progress, and provides personalized mentorship.

The school operates from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM, Monday to Saturday. Saturday afternoons are specifically dedicated to club activities, robotics, debate tournaments, and remedial support.

We provide an air-conditioned bus service across all major residential localities of Bhopal. Every bus is equipped with real-time GPS tracking accessible via the parent mobile portal, speed governors, CCTV cameras, first-aid kits, and trained female attendants.

We offer all three premier streams under the ISC board: Science (with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science), Commerce (with Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics), and Humanities (with Political Science, History, Psychology, Sociology, and Literature).
